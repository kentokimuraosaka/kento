Helping Japanese entrepreneurs build businesses and lives abroad.

Haiburi LLC is a U.S. (Wyoming) company operated from Osaka, Japan. We teach Japanese-speaking clients how to expand into the U.S. and form and run a U.S. LLC and how to prepare for relocation to Bali, Indonesia, through online courses and 1:1 consulting sessions.

Programs

All programs are delivered online in Japanese, combining recorded video lessons with live 1:1 sessions. Clients carry out their own applications; we provide education and preparation support.
